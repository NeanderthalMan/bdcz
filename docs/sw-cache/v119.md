# v119 — Home becomes Inbox, and Questions and PRs become a filter (bc-qzng7.2)

Phase 2 of the navigation redesign (bc-qzng7). Home is renamed **Inbox** and is always
the `human` sweep: the Human / Both / Agent switch is gone. The `Questions` and `PRs`
view pills leave the row and come back as one **Kind** filter pill beside the bead
search. Only Inbox carries a count. Each question or merge row gets a kind tag at the
head of its meta line, and a row at the top of Inbox counts the beads held for your
endorsement and links to `/endorse`.

Four files in `SHELL` change together, and three of them draw parts of one control:

| file | what is new |
|---|---|
| `public/inboxfilter.js` | the `kind` group (`KIND_TOKENS`), `kind` in the `home`, `question` and `pr` rows' `filters`, Home's label and icon, and `subSaid` no longer confessing a default on the unnarrowed Inbox |
| `public/viewbar.js` | the `question` and `pr` pills removed, `kinds: ['question', 'pr']` on the Inbox pill, `count` only on Inbox, and `aim` keyed on the Beads pill by id |
| `public/app.js` | `bootScope` pinned to `human`, the scope switch not mounted, `kindTagHtml` and the `kind-tag` click, `loadHeld` / `heldDoorHtml` and the `@held` chunk, and the empty state's new words |
| `public/style.css` | `.kind-tag`, its four colourings, and `.inbox-held` |

**A mixed pair breaks in a way nothing on screen reports.** A phone holding v118's
`inboxfilter.js` against v119's `viewbar.js` draws a row with no Questions or PRs pill
and no Kind filter either, so a question link (`?kind=question`) narrows the list with
no control anywhere to take the narrowing off. The other way round, v119's
`inboxfilter.js` against v118's `viewbar.js`, offers the same narrowing twice, once as
a pill and once as a filter. v118's `style.css` against v119's `app.js` draws the kind
tag as a bare grey button with browser chrome in every card's first line.
