# v116 — the PRs chip gets two subtabs: open PRs and the merge queue (bc-gjlix)

## What changed

`public/monitor.html` and `public/index.html` restructure the `#prs` section. It is a
`.prs-pane` now, holding a segmented switch (`#prs-tabs`) and two scrollers: `#prs-open`,
which is the board, and `#prs-queue`, which is the merge queue in the order it will try
each entry. `public/mergeboard.js` is new and is in `SHELL`. It owns the switch
(`window.beadcause.prsTabs`) and draws the queue from `GET /api/queues`, which now also
carries `bases`, the queue's last reading of every base. `public/prs.js` draws into
`#prs-open` and stands down while the queue subtab is up. `public/panelazy.js` injects
`mergeboard.js` between `monitor.js` and `prs.js`. `public/style.css` gained `.prs-pane`,
`.prs-tabs`, `.prs-tab-count` and the `.mq-*` rules.

## Which two files must not be mixed

**The documents and `prs.js`.** A new `monitor.html` or `index.html` served with a
cached `prs.js` from before this version gives an old board that looks up `#prs` and
writes its `innerHTML`. That wipes the switch and both lists and leaves the board as the
whole pane, with no way back to the queue. That direction breaks, so it owes the bump.
The other direction degrades instead: the new `prs.js` falls back to `#prs` when there is
no `#prs-open`, so it draws the board where it always did.

**The documents and `style.css`.** The new classes arrive in the same change as the
markup that uses them. With a cached stylesheet, `.prs-pane` has no flex rules and its
two scrollers do not take the pane's height, so the lists stop scrolling. The page looks
like it works while being broken, which is the case this version exists to prevent.
