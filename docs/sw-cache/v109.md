# v109 — the mark reads "bcz", one row, instead of four letters two over two (bc-uf4mc)

The app mark loses its d and becomes three letters in one row. `public/icon.svg` is
redrawn, and `public/icon-180.png` and `public/icon-512.png` are re-rastered from it with
`npm run icons`.

| half | what is new |
|---|---|
| `public/icon.svg` | b, c and z in one row, centred on (256,256) by their ink; farthest ink 167.2, inside the 205 safe zone |
| `public/icon-512.png`, `public/icon-180.png` | re-rendered from the new SVG (not in `SHELL`; they are fetched by the manifest and the page heads) |

**No mix of the two versions breaks anything.** The files are pictures that nothing reads
back, so v108's cached `icon.svg` under v109's pages only shows the old mark until the new
cache takes over. The bump exists so that the precached `/icon.svg` is replaced at all:
without it, the shell keeps serving the four-letter mark from cache indefinitely.

**`SHELL` is unchanged.** No file is added or renamed, so there is nothing new to precache.
