# v117 — the Questions pill splits, and the list can be ticked (bc-iaju1.5)

The inbox's `Questions` pill grows a second axis — **Asked | Held** — an **Order**
switch of its own, a count row at the top of the held half that leads to `/endorse`,
and a tick on every question card with a bulk *Set aside* over them. Three files in
`SHELL` change together and **two of them draw halves of the same control**, which is
why this needs a version rather than only a deploy.

| half | what is new |
|---|---|
| `public/inboxfilter.js` | the `asked` sub-filter on the `question` kind, with `fallback: ['asked']` and `single: true`; `qsort` named in that kind's `filters` |
| `public/app.js` | the `qsort` page group, `state.qsort` / `state.ticked`, the tick in `cardTopHtml`, the `.eq-bar` and `.triage-held` chunks, and `asideTicked()` |
| `public/style.css` | `.triage-held`, `.triage-held-n`, `.triage-held-go`, `.card-top .eq-pick`, `.card-top.has-tick`, `.board-btn.armed` |

**A mixed pair is a control with no layout, which is the failure the version exists to
prevent.** A phone holding v116's `style.css` against v117's `app.js` draws
`.triage-held` as an unstyled anchor — the way out of a ninety-bead backlog rendered as
a line of text — and draws the tick with no size, which on a `.card-top` that is
`justify-content: flex-end` puts a 0×0 target somewhere on the right of the card. The
page still works, so nothing on screen says the halves disagree.

The other direction is quieter and still wrong: v117's `style.css` against v116's
`app.js` styles nothing at all, and v116's `inboxfilter.js` against v117's `app.js`
offers a `qsort` group no pill names, which `adopt()` warns about in the console and
then never draws — an order switch the page believes it has.

**`SHELL` is unchanged.** No file is added or renamed, so a cached phone needs only the
precached three replaced.
